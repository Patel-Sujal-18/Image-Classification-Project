IMAGE-CLASSIFICATION-PROJECT


Dataset Source:
The dataset used in this project is derived from the Microsoft Cars vs Bikes dataset,
publicly available on Kaggle:

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/utkarshsaxenadn/car-vs-bike-classification-dataset

This dataset is commonly used for educational and research purposes in
computer vision and deep learning tasks.

--------------------------------------------------

Project Structure: It is the structure of the Image Classification project.

Image_Classification_Project/
├── dataset/
│   ├── train/
│   │   ├── Car/      -> Training images of Cars
│   │   └── Bike/      -> Training images of Bikes
│   │
│   └── test/
│       ├── Car/      -> Testing images of Cars
│       └── Bike/      -> Testing images of Bikes
│
├── car_bik_model.keras   -> Trained deep learning model
